Phase 1 prototype: this version has no backend. Nothing you enter is sent to a server. Demo data is kept only in your browser's local storage, and you can clear it anytime from My Profile → Settings.

1. Overview

Campus Connect ("we", "our") helps students, seniors and alumni of a campus connect with each other. This policy explains what information the platform collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number (optional) and password.
  • Profile information: role, department, batch, headline, bio, skills, links and preferences.
  • Verification documents: college email, ID card or certificate images, used only to confirm your campus affiliation.
  • Activity: connection requests, messages, saved items, applications and event registrations.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and display your profile to other campus members.
  • To verify that you belong to the campus community.
  • To enable connections, messaging and call requests.
  • To recommend relevant people, opportunities and events.

4. Sharing & visibility

Your public profile (name, headline, department, batch, skills) is visible to logged-in campus members. Your email and phone are shown only to people you are connected with. We never sell your personal information.

5. Storage & security

In Phase 2, data will be stored with industry-standard providers (e.g. Firebase / MongoDB) using encryption in transit (HTTPS). Verification documents will be stored securely and deleted after review.

6. Your rights

You can view, edit or delete your profile at any time. You can also request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by contacting us.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the contact page.
