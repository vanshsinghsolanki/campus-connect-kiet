General

What is Campus Connect?

Campus Connect is a platform that connects students with verified seniors and alumni from the same campus for mentorship, referrals, opportunities, learning resources and events.

Who can join?

Current students (juniors and seniors) and alumni of the college. Admin accounts will be introduced in Phase 2.

Is it free?

Yes, Campus Connect is completely free for students and alumni.

Account & verification

How do I create an account?

Go to Create account and complete the 3 short steps: account details, profile information and preferences.

Why should I verify my profile?

Verified profiles get a badge that shows you're genuinely from the campus. Seniors and alumni are much more likely to accept requests from verified members.

What verification methods are available?

College email (instant, with a one-time code), student/alumni ID card upload, or a degree/provisional certificate for alumni. Start verification.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Password reset will be available with real authentication in Phase 2. In the current demo, any password with 8+ characters works.

Connecting with people

How do I find a mentor?

Open People and filter by department, batch, skill or verification status. Open a profile to see what they're open to (mentorship, referrals, mock interviews…).

What's the difference between Connect, Message and Request call?

Connect sends a request with a short note. Message opens a chat window. Request call lets you propose a voice or video call with a date, time slot and topic.

When can I see someone's email?

Contact details are only visible after you're connected, to protect everyone's privacy.

Opportunities, resources & events

Who posts opportunities?

Seniors and alumni share internships, jobs, hackathons and certifications, usually from their own companies or communities.

Can I save items for later?

Yes. Tap the bookmark icon on any opportunity or resource. You'll find saved items under My Profile → Saved.

How do I add an event to my calendar?

Open any upcoming event and click Add to calendar. It downloads an .ics file that works with Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar.

About this version

Is my data stored on a server?

No. This is a Phase 1 front-end prototype. Profiles, opportunities, resources and events are sample data, and your demo activity is saved only in your browser's local storage.

What's coming in Phase 2?

Real authentication, a backend API and database, persisted connection requests, admin verification and real-time messaging. See the roadmap.
