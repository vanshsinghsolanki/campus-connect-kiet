About Campus Connect

Bridging juniors, seniors & alumni on one trusted platform.

Students often struggle to find the right senior or alumnus for guidance on placements, projects or higher studies. The help is out there, scattered across chat groups and social media. Campus Connect brings it together in one searchable, verified campus network.

Our values

What we stand for

Trust

Verification means you know you're talking to real people from your campus.

Community

Seniors and alumni give back, and juniors grow faster. Everyone benefits.

Access

Opportunities and resources available to everyone, not just the well-connected.

Privacy

Contact details are shared only after both people agree to connect.

Who it's for

Built for every stage of campus life

Student / Junior

Find guidance

Search, filter and view profiles. Register, get verified, connect with mentors, and join events.

Senior Student

Share experience

Show your profile, share notes, projects and internship leads with juniors.

Alumni

Give back

Mentor, refer and post opportunities from your company to your alma mater.

Roadmap

Where we are, and where we're going

Phase 1 · Delivered

Front-end prototype

  • Browse, search & filter profiles
  • Profile detail pages with connect / message / call demos
  • Registration, login & verification UI flows
  • Opportunities, resources & events with filters
  • Fully responsive, accessible, light & dark themes
Phase 2 · Planned

Full-stack platform

  • Real, persisted connection requests
  • Admin verification (approve / reject)
  • Real-time messaging
  • Authentication & secure file storage
  • Node.js / Express API with MongoDB or Firestore
